Service Fees & Quote Options

Project Manager Referral Fees

Business / Project Managers – Referral or Finder’s Fee (Light involvement)

If you simply research, vet, and refer a Project Manager — and the hiring business handles the contract, payments, and HR directly — a referral or finder’s fee model can apply.

Standard range: 10%–20% of the total contract value or first year’s pay (for longer-term placements).

Examples:

  • $60/hour contractor placed for 6 months (≈$62,400 total): your fee = $6,000–$12,000.
  • Or, charge a flat fee such as $2,000–$5,000 per successful placement depending on the role’s complexity.

This approach works well for project-based introductions and avoids payroll or liability overhead.
