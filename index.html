Why Hire a Project Manager with ALO?

As projects increase in size or complexity, costs can rise quickly without clear planning and coordination. Projects managed without dedicated oversight often experience 10–20% cost overruns due to scheduling delays, change orders, rework, and missed coordination.

ALO Project Management Services typically represents 8–10% of overall project cost and is focused on preventing those issues before they become expensive. By managing scope, timelines, contractors, permits, and quality from the start, ALO helps projects run smoother, reduce surprises, and often finish at a lower total cost than if managed independently.

Service Fees & Quote Options

Project Manager Referral Fees

Business / Project Managers – Referral or Finder’s Fee (Light involvement)

We research, vet, and refer a Project Manager — and your business handles the contract, payments, and HR directly — we typically recommend:

  • Standard range: 10%–20% of the total contract value or first year’s pay (for longer-term placements).

Examples:

  • $60/hour contractor placed for 6 months (≈$62,400 total): estimated fee range = $6,000–$12,000.
  • Or, a flat fee such as $2,000–$5,000 per successful placement depending on the role’s complexity.

This approach works well for project-based introductions and avoids payroll or liability overhead for both parties.

Connection & Navigation Services

Scope of Services

ALO Project Management Services, LLC provides connection and navigation support across all industries. Services may include identifying appropriate service providers, sharing contact information, explaining service options, and offering general guidance on next steps.

ALO does not act as a broker, service operator, dispatcher, or agent. All service agreements, scheduling, payments, and service delivery are handled directly between the client and the selected provider.

Fees

Connection and navigation services are typically billed at a flat fee ranging from $25 to $75 per request, depending on complexity and level of involvement. In certain simple situations, fees may be waived at ALO’s discretion. Any applicable fee will be communicated prior to billing.
